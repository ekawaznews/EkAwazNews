Severe Thunderstorm in Karachi with Lightning and Strong Winds Disrupts City Life

By Umer Javed | 18 March 2026 | 1 min read

A powerful thunderstorm with lightning, heavy rain, and strong winds has impacted Karachi, causing disruption in daily life across multiple areas.

Breaking: Karachi Thunderstorm 2026: Lightning, Heavy Rain & Strong Winds Hit the City

A sudden thunderstorm struck Karachi, bringing intense lightning, heavy rainfall, and strong winds that disrupted normal life in the city. Several areas reported fallen trees, damaged billboards, and water accumulation on roads, creating difficulties for commuters.

Residents experienced panic as thunder and lightning lit up the sky while powerful wind gusts swept through streets. Traffic flow was affected in multiple parts of the city, and reports of intermittent power outages added to the challenges faced by citizens.

According to the Meteorological Department, the current weather system is temporary; however, more rain and gusty winds are expected in the coming hours. Citizens have been advised to stay indoors, avoid unnecessary travel, and keep a safe distance from electric poles and weak structures.

Authorities and emergency services remain on alert to handle any developing situation and ensure public safety.
