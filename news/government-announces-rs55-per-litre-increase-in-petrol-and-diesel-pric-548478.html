Government Announces Rs55 Per Litre Increase in Petrol and Diesel Prices

By Umer Javed | 7 March 2026 | 2 min read

The federal government has announced a significant increase in petroleum product prices, raising petrol and high-speed diesel by Rs55 per litre. The revised prices will come into effect from midnight tonight.

🔴 Breaking: Government Announces Rs55 Per Litre Increase in Petrol and Diesel Prices

The federal government has approved a major increase in the prices of petroleum products, adding further pressure on consumers already facing rising living costs. According to an official notification issued by the relevant authorities, the price of petrol has been increased by Rs55 per litre, while the price of high-speed diesel (HSD) has also been raised by Rs55 per litre.

The new prices will take effect from 12:00 AM tonight, after which fuel stations across the country will begin selling petrol and diesel at the revised rates.

Government officials stated that the adjustment in petroleum prices was made in response to fluctuations in international oil markets as well as ongoing economic pressures. Authorities noted that Pakistan relies heavily on imported petroleum products, making domestic fuel prices sensitive to global oil trends and currency exchange rates.

Petrol is primarily used in motorcycles, rickshaws, and private vehicles, making it a key fuel for daily commuting. As a result, the increase in petrol prices is expected to directly affect millions of citizens who depend on personal transport for work and daily activities.

High-speed diesel, on the other hand, is widely used in the transportation and agriculture sectors, including trucks, buses, and farming machinery. An increase in diesel prices often leads to higher transportation costs, which can subsequently raise the prices of essential commodities and goods across the country.

Economic experts warn that rising fuel prices may contribute to further inflation, as transportation and logistics costs form a significant part of the supply chain. Businesses and consumers alike may face increased financial pressure as the cost of fuel impacts multiple sectors of the economy.

In recent years, Pakistan has experienced several adjustments in fuel prices due to global energy market volatility and domestic economic conditions. The government periodically reviews petroleum prices in line with international market movements and fiscal considerations.

The latest increase is expected to spark public debate, as citizens continue to grapple with the rising cost of living and economic uncertainty.
