About Ek Awaz News

Pakistan's Trusted Digital News Platform — ایک آواز نیوز

Who We Are

Ek Awaz News (ایک آواز نیوز — meaning "One Voice News") is one of Pakistan's fastest-growing digital news platforms, dedicated to delivering accurate, timely, and comprehensive news coverage to Pakistani readers at home and around the world.

We were built on a simple but powerful belief: every Pakistani deserves access to credible, unbiased, and up-to-date news. Whether you are in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, or anywhere across the globe, Ek Awaz News is your single trusted source for what matters most.

Our editorial team is led by Umer Javed, a senior journalist with extensive experience covering Pakistani politics, economy, crime, and international affairs. Every article published on Ek Awaz News is written or reviewed by our professional editorial team before going live.

Our Mission

Our mission is to be the single trusted voice (ایک آواز) that brings Pakistanis the news that matters — from breaking stories to in-depth analysis, from local weather to global affairs.

We are committed to responsible journalism: reporting facts accurately, giving voice to multiple perspectives, protecting the privacy of victims and vulnerable individuals, and holding power accountable through fair and fearless reporting.

We publish fresh, original content 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with automated systems ensuring our readers never miss a breaking story — no matter when it happens.

Our Editorial Standards

Ek Awaz News adheres to the highest standards of journalism ethics. We verify information before publishing, clearly label opinion and editorial content, issue corrections promptly when errors are made, and never publish content that could harm individuals or communities.

All crime reports are based exclusively on official public records, police statements, and verified government sources. We protect victim privacy and uphold the presumption of innocence for all accused persons until proven guilty in a court of law.

Weather reports are based on real-time data from official meteorological sources including WeatherAPI and the Pakistan Meteorological Department (PMD).

For corrections, complaints, news tips, or advertising inquiries, contact us at ekawaznews24@gmail.com.
